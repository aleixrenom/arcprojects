# Keywords
- **Expertise (topic)** - You may roll ability + stat instead of stat alone for checks involving the topic.
- **Strike (health/resolve)** - Roll ability and deal damage equal to the amount of successes rolled to the corresponding pool.
- **Empower** - Add your linked stat's dice pool to this ability's roll.
- **Spend X** - Temporarily remove that amount of dice from the ability's dice pool. By default you recover all of them after a Rest, but other effects may allow you to recover them earlier. If Spend triggers an effect that interacts with the ability's dice pool, the dice are spent after the effect has been resolved (for example if you have 2 dice and you Spend 1 to Strike, you roll 2 dice to Strike, then remove the one).
- **Soak X (health/resolve)** - Flatly reduce incoming damage to the named pool by X.
- **Mitigate (health/resolve)** - Roll ability and reduce incoming damage to the named pool by the amount of successes.
- **Evade (health/resolve)** - Roll ability + stat against a difficulty equal to the incoming damage against the named pool, then negate everything on a success, or take everything on a failure.

## Terminology
- **Roll (pool)** - Roll all the dice on the corresponding dice pool or dice pools together.
- **Stat OR Stat** - Whenever you get your first level in this ability, choose one of the two stats to be the linked stat for it.
- **Any stat** - Whenever you get your first level in this ability, choose any one stat to be the linked stat for it.

# Basic abilities
## Actions - aggression
### Martial adept
**Description:** You are proficient in direct combat.
**Stat:** Body
**Effect:** Empower Strike (health).

### Cutting words
**Description:** Your words cut deeper than any blade.
**Stat:** Mind
**Effect:** Empower Strike (resolve).

### Sense weakness
**Description:** You know how to read your opponent to know where and how to strike true.
**Stat:** Soul
**Effect:** Strike (health OR resolve).

## Actions - utility

### Talent
**Description:** You're an expert in one or more topics.
**Stat:** Any stat
**Effect:** Whenever you gain a level in this ability, gain Expertise in one new topic related to the linked stat. See the section on "topics" on the rules for more information. You may take this ability more than once, selecting a different stat every time.

## Actions - support

### Support
**Description:** You know how to reinforce someone else's actions effectively.
**Stat:** Any stat
**Effect:** While actively participating in a roll (check, Strike, etc.) with someone else, roll ability and add all successes to the amount of successes they get. If this triggers a close call that rewards an insight token, only the one who made the check obtains it. A roll can only be affected by one instance of Support at any given time.

### Lucky
**Description:** You seem to be able to get what you want when it really counts.
**Stat:** Any stat
**Effect:** Spend 1 to turn the result of a die rolled by anyone into a 1 or a 6.

### Healing
**Description:** You are proficient on treating wounds and soothing minds.
**Stat:** Mind OR Soul
**Effect:** Spend any number of dice to allow someone to recover that much Health or Resolve.

## Reactions

### Defensive
**Description:** You have some kind physical protection or are able to deflect part or all of incoming attacks.
**Stat:** Body
**Effect:** Soak (health) equal to half this ability's level rounded up. You may Spend 1 to Soak equal to full level instead.

### Iron will
**Description:** Through introspection or technique, you have learned to steel your mind against attacks.
**Stat:** Mind OR Soul
**Effect:** Soak (resolve) equal to half this ability's level rounded up. You may Spend 1 to Soak equal to full level instead.

### Dodge
**Description:** You know how to get out of harm's way at a moment's notice.
**Stat:** Body
**Effect:** Evade (health).

### Shrug off
**Description:** Through rationale or acceptance, you're able to dismiss incoming psychological damage.
**Stat:** Mind OR Soul
**Effect:** Evade (resolve).

### Counterattack
**Description:** You can return some of the hits taken.
**Stat:** Body
**Effect:** Mitigate (health) and note the number of successes rolled. You may add that number to the damage of the retaliate action that you take right after this reaction, as long as it's against the target that triggered this reaction.

### Slip away
**Description:** You can see the gaps in someone's aggression and can take advantage of them to get out of trouble.
**Stat:** Mind
**Effect:** Mitigate (health AND resolve), and then you are removed from the conflict.

### Stoic endurance
**Description:** You shrug off damage through sheer force of will.
**Stat:** Soul
**Effect:** Mitigate (health AND resolve), and then you can take the remaining damage in either your Health or Resolve pools distributed as you choose.

### Protect
**Description:** You know how to protect others from harm.
**Stat:** Body OR Soul
**Effect:** If an ally near you is going to take a reaction to avoid damage, you can use this reaction instead, and the attack will be directed at you. Mitigate (health AND resolve).

# Ability pack - Intrigue
Leverage, masks, and the cutting word. Winning people over, tearing them down, and not being read in return.

This pack includes one new keyword:
- **Veiled:** Veiled damage always deals exactly 1 Resolve damage, its source is unknown unless revealed through an ability, and cannot be answered by reactions. If the source of a Veiled effect is revealed before its damage connects, the damage is negated. Veiled damage does not trigger close calls.

## Temp idea place
It's hard to come up with mechanics that feel long-term and delayed using rulesets that are natively used for immediate resolutions. Regardless, here's my first idea, with keyword first and then abilities that could go with it. Analyse it, give your opinion, then give me pros and cons.

### Keyword
**Lever:** a Lever is a small piece of paper with some critical knowledge written on it (I will have to decide how precise it needs to be to be used). On top of a Lever you will put dice from different sources. As long as a player character or NPC knows about the knowledge written on the Lever, they can use the dice on top of said Lever for any roll narratively involving the knowledge. Only one Lever's dice can be used on a roll unless stated otherwise. The Lever may be removed for different narrative reasons, usually when the knowledge on it becomes public; if that happens, return all dice on it to their owners (if they were spent, they can now be recovered on the next Rest).

### Abilities
- **Short-term plans:** Once per scene when you come up with a plan, roll ability and add all successes to an existing or new Lever critical to that plan. Those dice will return to the ability at the end of the scene.
- **Long-term plans:** You may roll more than one Lever's dice if the roll involves more than one of them. To do so, Spend one die for every Lever you roll the dice of after the first.
- **Lever generation:** when learning about little-known critical knowledge, you may Spend any amount of dice, create a Lever, and put the spent dice on it.
- **Big payoff:** You may Spend any amount of dice the moment you (publicly?) expose a Lever, then roll the climactic roll or contested check. For each spent die, you can turn any of the Lever's dice rolled into a 6, or any opposed die rolled on a contested check to a 1.
- **Mask/Persona:** You may Spend any amount of dice to create a special Lever only you can use called a Mask, representing a persona you created or a practiced way of dealing with a particular group of people. You may use that Mask's dice with any checks to interact with people that honestly trust that Mask.
- **Impregnable:** When a Lever is used against you or one of your allies, you may roll ability. If the amount of successes equal or surpass the amount of dice on the Lever, that Lever is removed. You may instead Spend all of this ability's dice to get Empowered for that roll.
- **Rumours:** Spend any amount of dice to create an invented Lever. That Lever will be removed after its dice are rolled.

**Stat:** Mind
**Effect:** Strike (resolve). While in a public setting with many witnesses, this ability gains Empower. In that public setting, as long as you have the public's attention and a powerful topic of shame, after your Empower Strike, you may Spend any amount of dice, roll them, and deal all successes as additional damage. 

### Passive-aggressiveness (old)
**Description:** 
**Stat:** Mind
**Effect:** Strike (resolve). If used outside of a conflict, the target rolls a Mind check with difficulty equal to your failures rolled (all dice which are not successes). If they fail, you do not start a conflict due to this damage. If they succeed, conflict may start with this action being considered the first aggressive action.

### Passive-aggressiveness (new)
**Description:** 
**Stat:** Mind
**Effect:** A target you're interacting with rolls a Mind check, with a difficulty equal to this ability's level minus the amount of times that they have previously been affected by this ability. On a failure, they take a Veiled damage. On a success, they take no damage and recognise the aggression against them.

### Spread rumours
**Description:** 
**Stat:** Mind
**Effect:** Spend any amount of dice to spread a nasty rumour against someone. That person will take Veiled damage an amount of times equal to the dice spent after their next Rest.

### Imposing presence
**Description:** The mere existence of you nearby provokes shivers and worry to your would-be enemies.
**Stat:** Body OR Soul
**Effect:** Anyone of your choice in the same Scene as you rolls one less die for any Strike aimed at you or your allies. You may Spend any amount of dice to make someone in your Scene roll that many less dice on a roll they're making. During a conflict, if you Spend dice to reduce a roll, the number will be reduced from the next roll they make. A single person can only be affected by one instance of this ability at a time.
**Level 5:** You passively reduce rolls by two instead of one.

## Actions - utility
### Insider
**Description:** 
**Stat:** Mind OR Soul
**Effect:** When you gain this ability, choose a large group of people with similar characteristics, like a social class, a tribe, or a small society. You have Expertise when recalling information about this group, difficulty numbers when dealing with them are generally lower for you, and you know how to dress and act like them. You can take this ability multiple times, choosing a different group each time. 

### Persona
**Description:** You know how to act very differently depending on who you talk to, to the point of being unrecognisable. Or maybe you are an actor actually playing a role. 
**Stat:** Mind OR Soul
**Effect:** When you choose this ability, create a different personality, a different character for your character to play. You have Expertise when trying to convince someone that you are really that character. You may have an amount of these characters equal to half this ability's level rounded up. 

### Wealth
**Description:** You have coin, assets, or credit, and know how to make them talk.
**Stat:** Any stat
**Effect:** Spend any amount of dice to obtain goods, services, or access that money could plausibly buy. The GM sets the cost: 1 die for something a person of means could easily arrange, more for the rare, the urgent, or the discreet. You do not recover spent dice on a Rest. You recover all spent dice during Downtime.

### Connections
**Description:** People owe you, and you know exactly who and how much.
**Stat:** Mind OR Soul
**Effect:** Spend any amount of dice to call in a favour: someone you plausibly know acts on your behalf. The GM sets the cost by how much the favour asks of them: 1 die for a small kindness, more for risk, secrecy, or real sacrifice. You do not recover spent dice on a Rest. You recover all spent dice during Downtime.

## Actions - versatile

### Arcanist
**Description:** You are adept in the ways of magic and can bend it to create many surprising effects.
**Stat:** Mind
**Effect:** 
- You have Expertise when interacting with magical effects or objects. 
- Prestidigitation - You can create instantaneous, harmless sensory effects out of thin air, such as a shower of sparks, a puff of wind, faint musical notes, or an odd odour.
**Level 2:** 
- Magic Missile - Strike (health)
- Minor Illusion - You can create sounds or images out of thin air, but not both at the same time. The sound can't be too loud, and the image is stationary, only visual, and has to fit within a 2m³ area. 
**Level 3:**
- Mage Armor - Spend 1 to gain Soak 1 (health) until the next Rest. This effect does not stack.
- Shockwave - Spend 1 to Strike (health) three targets that are near one another.
**Level 4:** 
- Mind Spike - Spend 1 to Strike (resolve)
- Fabricate - Using magic, you can slowly turn raw material into fabricated objects, or fabricated objects into raw materials. The rate is around 1 hour per cubic meter. You may Spend 1 to double the rate until the next Rest, and this doubling stacks multiplicatively.
**Level 5:**
- Resourceful - Spend 1 from any of your dice pools to recover one spent die from any one of your other dice pools.
- Actually - Spend 1 to gain Expertise in one Mind topic until the end of the scene.

### Telekinesis
**Description:** You can move things with your mind.
**Stat:** Mind
**Effect:** Treat ability + stat as your effective Body for moving weight with this ability (weight guidelines can be found in the Body section of the rules). You can move an amount of targets equal to this ability's level, that are within a distance in meters equal to twice this ability's level. You can only move inanimate objects. Combine all object's weights to see how much you can move.
**Level 2:** 
- Force blast - Strike (health)
**Level 3:** You can now move willing living beings, including yourself.
**Level 4:** 
- Kinetic grip - Spend 1 to try to move an unwilling living being outside of conflicts. Roll ability with Empower for contested checks when they try to resist.
**Level 5:** 
- Exaltation of force - Spend 1 to double the amount of weight, objects, and distance of this ability until the end of the Scene. This effect does not stack.
**Level 6:** 
- Crush - Empower Strike (health)

### Theriomancer
**Description:** You can bond with beasts and transform into them. 
**Stat:** Soul
**Effect:** 
- You may transform into a bestial shape whenever you want (no need to actively use this as an Action). At this level, when you transform, you adopt slight bestial features, like claws, fangs, horns, etc. This transformation will become more prominent through the levels.
- You have Expertise when dealing with animals. 

You may roll ability + stat instead of stat for checks directly involving animals. You can also transform into a beast at will, but the beast you transform into needs to be more or less your size. While transformed your Body and Soul stats swap, changing your Health and Resolve pools if necessary, and this ability's linked stat becomes Body instead of Soul. While transformed, this ability has Strike (health). 
**Level 3:** The beast you transform into can be half or double your size.
**Level 5:** The beast can be a quarter or triple your size.
**Level 7:** The beast can be minuscule or four times your size. 

### Elementalist
**Description:** Through will, guile, or sheer discipline, you learned to bend the elements to your will.
**Stat:** Any stat
**Effect:** You can manipulate earth, fire, water, and air at will. You can move and reshape the element with precision as long as it's near you, it exists, and is in its natural state (except for fire, which you can generate first before manipulating). At this level you can only manipulate 10cm³ of material, but this amount will increase with the ability's level as seen below, together with gaining new capabilities. You also gain Expertise in elemental magic.
**Level 2:** You can manipulate 30cm³.
**Level 3:** You can manipulate 1m³. This ability gains Strike (health).
**Level 4:** You can manipulate 2m³.
**Level 5:** You can manipulate 3m³.
**Level 6:** You can manipulate 5m³. This ability gains Empower.
**Level 7:** You can manipulate 7m³.
**Level 8:** You can manipulate 10m³.

## Actions - aggression

### Summonner
**Description:** Create new temporary beings, living or not. 
**Stat:** Mind OR Soul
**Effect:** Spend any number of dice to create a new being (hereby called "summon"). When you get this ability, decide on what kind of summon it is (undead, elementals, animated objects...). The summon always has the following characteristics:
- All stats start at 0.
- They have a Health pool of 1 + their Body stat, and a Resolve pool of 1 + their Mind OR Soul stat. 
- They have a size between your own and half of that. 
- They can do checks as normal as long as they have at least 1 level in the corresponding stat.
- They have Strike (health) using Body and Strike (resolve) using Mind or Soul, treating their stats as abilities for this purpose. 
- You only recover the dice spent to create them when they die or you dismiss them.
- They cannot trigger close calls.

When you get this ability, choose two of the following customisations. From then on, for each die you Spend while summoning, choose one of the chosen customisations, and the summon will receive the benefit.
- +1 Body
- +1 Soul
- +1 Mind
- More size
- Less size
- More speed
- Can fly
- Can easily climb any surface
- Can easily burrow through loose terrain
- Soak +1 (health)
- Soak +1 (resolve)
You may choose another customisation option at ability level 3 and every 2 levels after that.

### Teleportation
**Description:** You can disappear from where you are and appear in a nearby spot in the blink of an eye.
